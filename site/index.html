Method

A simple model with stated limits.

Fixed fees are subtracted once. Percentage costs are calculated from the original amount and use exact integer arithmetic rather than floating-point currency math.

Net received amount − fixed fees − amount × percentage fees
What this calculator does not model
  • Taxes, price movement, staking yield or investment return.
  • Fees charged in a different asset than the amount sent.
  • Minimum withdrawals, network congestion or counterparty risk.
  • Whether a specific region or account can use a route.
