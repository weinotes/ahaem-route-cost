## Show HN draft

Title: Show HN: Open-source crypto transfer cost calculator

Body:

I kept seeing transfer fees split across withdrawal fees, network costs and
percentage-based platform or conversion charges. Headline numbers made it hard
to understand the amount that would actually arrive.

I built a small calculator that models those components with integer arithmetic
and keeps every assumption visible. It runs entirely in the browser, has no
wallet connection and contains no analytics or affiliate parameters.

I would appreciate feedback on the calculation model, especially fee types that
should be represented separately. The source is Apache-2.0.
